## 1. CRITICAL DESIGN OVERHAUL: SOLVING THE "DARK MODE" PROBLEM

### 1.1 The User Critique: How to Do Luxury Dark Mode Properly
*User Feedback:* "Some designs look very bad for that black color. I don't think the real estate website should look like a dark mode. If it will be in dark mode, how it should look like? A professional client can see it and say it is a great website and he wants to buy that website."

#### Why Flat Dark Mode Fails in Real Estate:
When developers use pitch black `#000000` with high-saturation neon lines, the website looks like a wireframe video game or developer terminal. High-end buyers do not want to feel like they are debugging code; they want to feel like they are inside a private luxury penthouse at twilight.

#### The Architectural Solution: Nocturnal Spatial Luxury
1. **Never Pure Black (`#000000`):** Replaced with **Deep Obsidian Gunmetal (`#0C1017`)** and **Nocturnal Slate (`#141C27`)**. These colors have soft blue-gray undertones that mimic an architectural exhibition hall illuminated by focused gallery spotlights.
2. **Atmospheric Lighting:** The 3D viewport uses a radial vignette gradient (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 45%, #0F1E33 0%, #06090E 85%)`), giving the physical sensation that the 3D building model is sitting on an illuminated glass pedestal.
3. **Refined Metallic & Cyan Accents:** Replaces harsh neon with **Spatial Cyan (`#38BDF8`)** and **Soft Electric Violet (`#818CF8`)**, paired with **Champagne Gold (`#C5A059`)** for pricing and guide highlights.
4. **Soft Text:** Typography is rendered in **Warm Alabaster (`#F1F5F9`)** and **Muted Tech Greige (`#94A3B8`)** to completely eliminate optical vibration.
