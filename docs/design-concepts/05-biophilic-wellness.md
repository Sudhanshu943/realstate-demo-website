# 05 — Biophilic Wellness Living

- **Audience:** Families and buyers motivated by landscape, health, low-density living, and sustainability.
- **Concept:** Homes framed through light, air, trees, and daily rituals—not generic “eco” badges.
- **Palette:** Pine `#244236`, moss `#71856B`, clay `#B46D4D`, oat `#F3EFE4`.
- **Typography:** Humanist serif for 56–88 px headlines; rounded humanist sans for 16–18 px body.
- **Layout:** Organic image crops, soft section transitions, wellness feature bands, landscape stories.
- **Motion:** Slow ambient drift and crossfades; always reduced-motion safe.
- **Conversion:** Book a site walk, explore landscape plan, ask about verified sustainability features.
- **Best for:** Foothill, gated community, wellness, retirement, and second-home projects.
- **Risk:** Never invent green certifications or health claims.
- **Tools:** Next.js, image focal-point CMS, lightweight scroll motion, verified certification data.
