# 02 — Monochrome Editorial

- **Audience:** Design-conscious buyers and boutique/luxury agencies.
- **Concept:** A property journal rather than a portal—bold photography, issue-style numbering, and fashion-editorial pacing.
- **Palette:** Ink `#111111`, white `#F7F7F3`, signal red `#D3472F` used sparingly.
- **Typography:** High-contrast serif/Didone for 72–128 px headlines; neutral grotesk for 13–16 px metadata.
- **Layout:** Asymmetric image grid, oversized title, thin rules, generous white space, editorial captions.
- **Motion:** Page-reveal masks, image crop shifts, underline transitions.
- **Conversion:** “Arrange a private viewing” after the story; minimal search chrome.
- **Best for:** Low-volume, high-consideration inventory with excellent photography.
- **Risk:** Can feel inaccessible or sparse if listing facts are hidden. Keep facts and CTA visible.
- **Tools:** Next.js, CSS grid, Motion for sophisticated transitions, CMS for journal stories.
