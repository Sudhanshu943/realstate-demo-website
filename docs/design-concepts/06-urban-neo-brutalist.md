# 06 — Urban Neo-brutalist

- **Audience:** Younger urban buyers, creative commercial tenants, and challenger brokerages.
- **Concept:** Direct, bold, unapologetically digital—high contrast, thick borders, oversized type.
- **Palette:** Black `#050505`, electric yellow `#F2FF3D`, warm white `#F5F2EA`, cobalt `#3455FF`.
- **Typography:** Condensed/black grotesk at 72–140 px; monospace metadata at 13–15 px.
- **Layout:** Hard-edged cards, ticker bands, diagonal labels, visible controls, zero ornamental luxury cues.
- **Motion:** Snappy 120–180 ms transforms, marquee/ticker with pause controls, cursor-follow only on capable devices.
- **Conversion:** Immediate availability, price, WhatsApp, book-now interaction.
- **Best for:** Distinctive city apartments, co-working, retail, student housing.
- **Risk:** Bold design must retain legibility, focus states, and clear hierarchy.
- **Tools:** CSS variables, Motion for controlled micro-interactions, no heavy 3D required.
