# 08 — Heritage Estate

- **Audience:** Buyers of historic homes, estates, farmhouses, and legacy properties.
- **Concept:** Archival elegance with modern clarity—provenance, craft, and stewardship.
- **Palette:** Burgundy `#542B32`, parchment `#F0E7D2`, olive `#585F43`, brass `#B08A55`.
- **Typography:** Old-style serif at 54–92 px; small caps and restrained serif body at 17–19 px.
- **Layout:** Framed imagery, ornamental rules, timeline/provenance blocks, room-by-room narrative.
- **Motion:** Gentle dissolves and page-turn-like transitions; no glossy app effects.
- **Conversion:** Request particulars, private appointment, heritage specialist contact.
- **Best for:** Scarce, story-rich inventory.
- **Risk:** Verify dates, ownership, conservation status, and historical claims.
- **Tools:** CMS with source fields, high-resolution IIIF-style imagery if needed, PDF particulars.
