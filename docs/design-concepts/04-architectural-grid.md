## 2. LUXURY COLOR CHEMISTRY & MATERIAL PALETTE

### 2.1 Color Matrix (Swiss Engineering Blueprint)
- **Drafting Slate Canvas (`#18324A` or `#121C26`):** Deep technical blue-gray reminiscent of high-end CAD software and historic cyanotype blueprints.
- **Technical Drawing Grid Parchment (`#D9D7D0`):** Used in light contrast cards to simulate archival drafting vellum.
- **Drafting Orange (`#F26A2E`):** High-precision safety orange used for grid coordinates, dimension callouts, and structural warnings.
- **Blueprint Line Cyan (`#38BDF8`):** Laser-thin vector lines illustrating ducting, conduit, and beam spans.
- **Muted Steel Gray (`#8898A8`):** Secondary annotations and specification values.

### 2.2 Rejection of Cheap Styling
- **Exposed Structural Grids:** Grid lines (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) are deliberately made visible, creating an authentic drafting aesthetic.
- **Corner Measurement Ticks:** Every card and blueprint viewport features architectural corner registration marks (`+` and `L` ticks).
